# 首批城市主图生成提示记录

生成模式：从零生成，不使用参考图。每座城市一个独立调用。

## 共用母版提示

> Create one production-ready original city image asset for the “山河有应” mobile web product. Asset use: city-detail hero image and share-card crop master. Visual type: photorealistic editorial cityscape, newly generated and not imitating any identifiable existing photograph. Composition: 3:2 landscape master designed to survive a 4:3 and vertical mobile crop; keep the landmark cluster within the central 70% with generous safe margins, and use clear foreground, middle ground and background depth. Brand art direction: about 70% authentic city presence and 30% subtle “山河有应” atmosphere, with deep ink-green and near-black shadows, restrained dark-gold highlights, refined cinematic haze and extremely sparse dust-like glints. Restrictions: no embedded text, logo, watermark, decorative frame, map graphics, zodiac symbols, busy star field, complex glowing orbit lines, excessive gold, identifiable faces, invented landmarks, impossible geography, cyberpunk neon, illustration or painterly style. Quality: crisp, high-resolution and clean enough for a premium public launch asset. Output a consistent 3:2 landscape image.

## 城市差异提示

- London：Thames、Tower Bridge、克制的伦敦天际线；阴云傍晚与轻微金色天光。
- Vancouver：Burrard Inlet、downtown、North Shore Mountains、常绿植被；雨后海洋性空气。
- Tokyo：Tokyo Tower、密集但有序的现代天际线、湾岸或河流层次；蓝调时刻，避免霓虹过载和寺庙拼贴。
- Singapore：Marina Bay、Marina Bay Sands、热带植被；湿润黄昏和克制水面金光。
- New York：Lower Manhattan、Brooklyn Bridge、East River；雨后晚光。
- Barcelona：Sagrada Família、Eixample 城市肌理、地中海与丘陵只在合理地理关系中出现；干暖傍晚。
- Shanghai：Huangpu River、Bund、Pudong skyline，包含 Oriental Pearl Tower 与 Shanghai Tower 的合理位置；湿润蓝调时刻。
- Kyoto：Yasaka Pagoda、Higashiyama 屋顶和东山；小雨后的安静傍晚，避免拥挤与樱花陈词滥调。
